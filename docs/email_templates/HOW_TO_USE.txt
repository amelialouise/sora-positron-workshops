SORA Workshop Email Templates — How to Use
============================================

Files in this folder
--------------------
- cohort_viable.md   — sent to each viable cohort (one send per cohort)
- cohort_waitlist.md — sent to all non-viable / waiting-list registrants

Copy-paste workflow (Gmail or Outlook web)
------------------------------------------
1. Open the template on GitHub in the browser (NOT the raw view).
   The rendered view shows formatted headers, bold text, and the
   open-seats table. The raw view shows literal **markdown** syntax,
   which will paste in ugly.

2. Select the email body text on the rendered page (everything from
   the subject line down to the SORA EC sign-off).

3. Copy (Cmd/Ctrl-C) and paste into the compose window in Gmail or
   Outlook web. Both accept rich-text paste and will preserve the
   formatting.

4. Replace every {{placeholder}} before sending. Common ones:
     {{N}}              — cohort number
     {{Day, Month D}}   — scheduled date
     {{time}} {{tz}}    — start/end time and time zone
     {{reply-by date}}  — deadline for replies
     {{org}}            — GitHub org slug in the FAQ link
     {{slots_available}} (waitlist email only) — open seats per cohort

5. Put the subject line in the Subject field, then move all
   recipient addresses into the BCC field (NOT To/Cc) to protect
   everyone's email privacy.

Where the recipient list and seat counts come from
--------------------------------------------------
Both come from the CSV exported by the Cohort Builder app via the
"Download Cohort Assignments" button (workshop_cohorts_<date>.csv).

Viable cohort email:
  - For each cohort_number > 0, BCC every email in that group.
  - One send per cohort_number.

Waitlist email:
  - BCC every email where cohort_number < 0 (the non-viable rows).
  - For the "open seats" table, list one row per cohort_number > 0:
    use cohort_number, time_slot, and slots_available.
  - Skip any cohort where slots_available == 0.

Reminders before hitting send
-----------------------------
- Double-check that recipient addresses are in BCC, not To/Cc.
- Re-read the email after pasting — Outlook in particular sometimes
  mangles spacing on tables; tweak as needed.
- Never paste registration data (names, agencies, etc.) into the
  email body. Only the recipient's own email goes in BCC.
